Information Technology at KCC

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology develops students for software development, networking, database systems, cybersecurity, and information technology services.

Actual course sequences, subjects, schedules, and requirements are determined through official KCC academic publications.

What You'll Learn

  • Programming fundamentals and software development
  • Database design and information management
  • Computer networks and systems administration
  • Web and mobile application development
  • Systems analysis and project planning
  • Information security and risk awareness
  • Testing, deployment, and technology support
  • Professional, ethical, and team-based practice

Program Overview

KCC academic programs combine foundational knowledge with practical learning.

Students develop discipline-specific competencies while strengthening communication, critical thinking, ethical judgment, and service to the community.

Hands-On Learning

Learners practice skills through projects, laboratories, workshops, and community experiences.

The curriculum encourages collaboration and meaningful application of theory in real settings appropriate to each academic field.

Career Preparation

Programs prepare graduates for professional opportunities and further studies.

Students build industry awareness, employability skills, research competence, and the confidence to adapt in a changing workplace.

Faith and Service

Catholic formation and community engagement remain central to the KCC experience.

Students learn to lead responsibly, practice compassion, and respond thoughtfully to social and community needs.

Student Support

Learners receive guidance from faculty and student-support services.

KCC encourages academic advising, personal development, co-curricular participation, and a welcoming campus community.
