A Christ-centered learning community committed to academic excellence, faith formation, and meaningful service since 1927.

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College serves learners at every stage of their educational journey in Kabankalan City.

  • Quality and inclusive Catholic education rooted in the Gospel.
  • Academic, spiritual, social, and personal growth for every learner.
  • Graduate formation for effective leadership and community transformation.
