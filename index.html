A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served as a home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City for nearly a century.

  • Academic excellence grounded in critical thinking and purpose.
  • Faith formation through Catholic tradition and service.
  • Holistic development preparing students to lead and transform communities.
